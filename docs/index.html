    Install on your Mac

    1. Download and unzip DiskManager-macOS.zip.
    2. Move DiskManager.app to Applications and open it.
    3. Choose a drive, or drop a folder onto the window, to start a scan.

    The app is ad-hoc signed and not notarized by Apple. If macOS blocks the first launch, check that the zip came from this project, then follow Apple’s instructions.

    Questions

    Does it upload my files?

    No. File names, paths and sizes are processed on your Mac. The app has no analytics and uploads no scan results.

    What happens when I delete something?

    Items sent to the Trash free up space once you empty the Trash. Permanent deletion cannot be undone, so the app asks you to choose it explicitly.

    Why does macOS block it the first time?

    The download is ad-hoc signed and not notarized. Check the checksum and that the zip came from this project’s releases before you allow the app to open.

    Does it need administrator access?

    Only for an administrator scan or when removing a snapshot. The app asks first.

    Which Macs does it run on?

    macOS 14 Sonoma or later, on Apple silicon and Intel Macs.
